Mo:Mo

Description

The most classic style, these dumplings are carefully pleated by hand and steamed to perfection, usually filled with seasoned minced meat or chopped vegetables. They are traditionally served with a vibrant, spicy tomato chutney or sesame-based dipping sauce known as achar.

Ingridents

  • All-purpose flour (Maida): 2 cups
  • Salt: ½ to 1 teaspoon
  • Oil: 1 to 2 tablespoons
  • Lukewarm water: As needed to form a smooth
  • Ground/Minced meat: 1 to 2½ pounds
  • Onions: 1 cup, finely chopped
  • Cilantro / Coriander leaves: 1 cup, fresh and chopped
  • Ginger & Garlic: 2 to 4 tablespoons each, minced or made into a paste
  • Spices: Ground cumin, coriander powder, turmeric, salt, and black pepper
  • Fat/Oil: 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil, ghee, or butter to keep the meat moist

Steps

  1. Mix Dry Dough: In a large bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour (maida) and salt until evenly combined.
  2. Knead the Dough: Gradually pour in the lukewarm water along with the oil, kneading the mixture for 8 to 10 minutes until a smooth, firm, and pliable dough forms.
  3. Rest the Dough: Shape the dough into a ball, cover it completely with a damp cloth or plastic wrap, and let it rest for 30 minutes to relax the gluten.
  4. Mix the Aromatics: In a separate large bowl, combine the minced chicken, finely chopped onions, fresh cilantro, and the ginger-garlic paste.
  5. Season and Moisten Filling: Add the ground cumin, coriander powder, turmeric, salt, and black pepper, then pour in the 2 tablespoons of oil, ghee, or butter to keep the meat juicy.
  6. Marinate the Meat: Mix the filling thoroughly with your hands for 2 minutes to bind the ingredients, then let it rest for 15 minutes so the flavors develop.
  7. Portion the Dough: Divide your rested dough into small, equal, marble-sized balls (about 1 inch in diameter) and keep them covered so they do not dry out.
  8. Roll the Wrappers: Take a dough ball, dust it lightly with flour, and roll it out into a 3-to-4-inch circle, ensuring the edges are thinner than the center.
  9. Add the Filling: Place exactly one tablespoon of the marinated chicken mixture right into the center of your rolled wrapper.
  10. Pleat and Seal: Lift the edges, pinch a small fold, and continue creating overlapping pleats in a circle, twisting and sealing them together tightly at the top.
  11. Prep the Steamer: Oil your steamer trays thoroughly to prevent sticking, then arrange the momos on the trays leaving half an inch of space between each one.
  12. Steam the Momos: Bring water to a rolling boil in the base pot, place the trays on top, cover tightly, and steam on medium-high heat for 10 to 12 minutes until the wrappers look translucent and are no longer sticky.
