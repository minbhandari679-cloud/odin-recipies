Cookie

A cookie is a small, sweet, baked food—typically flat, crisp, or soft—made primarily from flour, sugar, and fat (such as butter or oil), often with added ingredients like eggs, chocolate chips, nuts, or raisins

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Fat
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Leaveners
  • Salt
  • Flavorings

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat to keep the cookies from sticking.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set this dry mixture aside.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and white sugar together until the mixture is smooth, creamy, and slightly fluffy.
  4. Beat the egg and vanilla extract into the creamed butter mixture until completely combined.
  5. Pour the dry flour mixture into the wet ingredients. Mix gently with a wooden spoon or spatula just until the flour disappears.
  6. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto your prepared baking sheet. Leave about 2 inches of space between each dough ball, as they will spread while baking.
  7. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown but the centers still look slightly soft.
  8. Remove from the oven and let the cookies sit on the hot baking sheet for 5 minutes (they will finish setting up). Transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.
