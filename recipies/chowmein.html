Chowmein

Chowmein is a popular stir-fried noodle dish made with wheat noodles, vegetables, soy sauce, and aromatics, often cooked quickly over high heat for a savory, flavorful meal

Ingridents

  • Noodles
  • Oil
  • Vegetables
  • Soy Sauce
  • Garlic and Ginger
  • Salt
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles according to the package instructions until just cooked. Drain well and toss with a drop of oil to keep them from sticking.
  2. In a small bowl, mix together the soy sauce, sesame oil, and any additional seasonings. Set this sauce mixture aside.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat until hot.
  4. Add the minced garlic and ginger, stir-frying for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. Add the sliced vegetables to the wok and stir-fry briskly for 2 to 3 minutes until tender-crisp.
  6. Toss the cooked noodles into the wok with the vegetables.
  7. Pour the sauce mixture over the noodles and vegetables, tossing constantly for 1 to 2 minutes until everything is evenly coated and heated through.
  8. Remove from heat and serve warm, garnished with green onions if desired.
