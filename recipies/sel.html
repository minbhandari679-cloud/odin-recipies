Sel Roti

Sel Roti is a traditional Nepalese sweet, ring-shaped rice bread, made primarily from soaked rice batter, sugar, ghee, and spices, fried to a golden crisp on the outside and soft on the inside

Ingridents

  • Rice
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Milk
  • Cardamom
  • Cloves
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Soak coarse rice in water for 4 to 6 hours, then drain completely and grind it into a slightly coarse paste.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the ground rice, melted ghee, sugar, pounded cardamom, and cloves.
  3. Add milk gradually and beat the mixture vigorously until a smooth, pourable yet thick batter is formed. Cover and let it rest for 1 to 2 hours.
  4. Heat oil or ghee in a wide, flat-bottomed deep frying pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour the batter by hand or using a funnel into a ring shape directly into the hot oil.
  6. Fry the ring for 1 to 2 minutes until the bottom side turns golden brown and firm.
  7. Flip the ring using long wooden sticks (jhir) and fry the other side until golden brown and crispy.
  8. Remove the sel roti from the hot oil, drain excess oil on paper towels, and allow to cool slightly before serving.
